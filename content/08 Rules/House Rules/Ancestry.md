# Ancestry

> [!summary]
> players get a small benefit based on their ancestry 

---

## Rule

# 🧬 Ancestry Table (d8)

These traits represent inherited qualities layered on top of your character’s race.

---

1. **Hardened Blood**  
   *Benefit:* Advantage on saving throws against disease.

2. **Keen-Eyed Lineage**  
   *Benefit:* +1 bonus to Perception checks.

3. **Iron Stomach**  
   *Benefit:* Once per long rest, gain resistance to poison damage for 1 minute.

4. **Swift Reflexes**  
   *Benefit:* Once per short rest, increase your movement speed by 10 ft for one turn.

5. **Thick Skin**  
   *Benefit:* +1 maximum HP per level.

6. **Night-Bred**  
   *Benefit:* Gain darkvision 30 ft (or +30 ft if already possessed).

7. **Silver Tongue**  
   *Benefit:* Advantage on one Persuasion check per long rest.

8. **Arcane Touched**  
   *Benefit:* Learn one cantrip from any class spell list.

---

## Procedure

1. Roll D8 
2. player notes down the benefit on the character sheet.
3. DM plans on how they will kill off the character if they get a really good benefit.
