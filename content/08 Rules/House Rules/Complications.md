## Rule


# ⚠️ Complications Table (d12)

Each complication grants a meaningful benefit and a drawback.

| d12 | Complication         | Benefit                                                                                                          | Drawback                                                                                                         |
| --- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Elemental Absorption | +6 max HP at 1st level, +1 per level thereafter. Gain resistance to one elemental damage type                    | When reduced to 0 HP, the elemental takes control; attack nearest creature and automatically fail one death save |
| 2   | Sacrificial Aegis    | Choose a creature type. Advantage on saves vs that type. Once per long rest, drop to 1 HP instead of 0 from them | Creatures of that type can sense or track you more easily                                                        |
| 3   | Devil’s Bargain      | Once per long rest, add 1d6 to an attack, save, or check                                                         | On a natural 1, the DM gains an Infernal Favor                                                                   |
| 4   | Arcane Instability   | +1 Spell Save DC                                                                                                 | On a natural 1 while casting, roll on a Wild Magic Surge table                                                   |
| 5   | Marked by Death      | Advantage on death saving throws                                                                                 | Disadvantage on first save vs necrotic damage each combat                                                        |
| 6   | Oathbound            | +1 AC while acting in alignment with your oath                                                                   | Breaking your oath removes all Heroic Inspiration until atonement                                                |
| 7   | Cursed Bloodline     | Learn one additional cantrip                                                                                     | Disadvantage on saves vs charm (fey or fiends)                                                                   |
| 8   | Shadow-Touched       | Gain darkvision 60 ft (or +30 ft). Once per long rest, become invisible until end of next turn                   | Bright sunlight imposes disadvantage on first attack each combat                                                 |
| 9   | Vengeful Spirit      | Once per long rest, when hit, deal 1d8 psychic damage to attacker                                                | On a 1 during long rest roll, gain one level of exhaustion                                                       |
| 10  | Doomed Prophecy      | Once per session, reroll any d20                                                                                 | Automatically fail first death save when reduced to 0 HP                                                         |
| 11  | Hunter’s Marked      | Advantage on Survival checks to track creatures                                                                  | A powerful hunter tied to your past may pursue you                                                               |
| 12  | Primordial Scar      | Resistance to force damage                                                                                       | When taking force damage, roll d6; on 1 you are knocked prone                                                    |

---

## Procedure

1. roll a D12
2. player notes the benefit on the character sheet
3. DM notes the drawback on the character notes
